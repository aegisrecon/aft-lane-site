PT Lane Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Aegis Recon

Summary

PT Lane does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. This policy is published by Aegis Recon and takes effect on 14 November 2026.

What the app stores

The names, unit details, ages, test scores and saved tests you enter are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are not sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

Timers, your grading group and your app settings are stored the same way.

Files you export

A scorecard PDF or a results sheet is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send or save it. The app removes its temporary copy afterwards.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The app learns only which product is active on your Apple Account so it can unlock features. It never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

Use About, then Erase Soldiers and saved tests, to remove everything you have entered. Deleting the app from your device also removes all of it.

Children

PT Lane is intended for Soldiers and those who grade or train them. It is not directed to children under 13.

Not an official record

PT Lane is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army or any part of the U.S. Government. Information you enter is not shared with the Army.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update and its effective date will change. Questions can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
