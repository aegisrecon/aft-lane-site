PT Lane Support

Aegis Recon

Contact

E-mail support@aegisrecon.ai. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what you were doing.

Restore a purchase

Open Tools, then About, and tap Restore purchases. The same button is on the Pricing screen. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Cancel or change a subscription

Open Tools, then Pricing, and tap Manage subscription, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Pro and Unit renew automatically until canceled.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Erase what you entered

Open Tools, then About, and tap Erase Soldiers and saved tests. Deleting the app also removes everything.

The scorecard

The DA Form 705-AFT that PT Lane fills in is a reproduction for you to check against the current edition. It is not an official record until it is signed.

Independent app

PT Lane is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the U.S. Army or any part of the U.S. Government. Your unit's signed DA Form 705-AFT is the score of record.

Privacy

PT Lane does not collect personal information. Read the privacy policy.
